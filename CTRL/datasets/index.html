Read this before you load anything

  • mislabelledProsperity 2 round 2: the three files named prices_round_2_day_*.csv hold ORCHIDS observations in one wide table, not an order book.
  • re-releasedSome days ship twice. Prosperity 3 round 4 day 3 repeats round 3 day 3; round 5 days 2 and 3 repeat round 4 (prices and observations); Prosperity 4 round 4 days 1 and 2 repeat round 3. Count each day once.
  • editionThe Library catalogue files under “Prosperity guide” are Prosperity 4 round 1 data: they have the same git blob hashes as the round 1 files in a public Prosperity 4 backtester.
  • namesBuyer and seller names appear in Prosperity 2 round 5 (the _withname files for rounds 1, 3 and 4), Prosperity 3 round 5 and Prosperity 4 round 4. Elsewhere they are blank.
  • separatorsPrices and trades use semicolons. Prosperity 3 observations use commas.
  • empty sidesA book side can be empty; mid_price then comes from the other side alone. Filter or backfill before using it as a price.
  • communityProsperity 4 tutorial and rounds 2 to 5 come from a community backtester repository, pinned to one commit. Their provenance has not been checked against IMC's own downloads.
  • currencyTrades are quoted in SEASHELLS in Prosperity 1 to 3 and in XIRECS in Prosperity 4.

File schemas
filecolumns
pricesday, timestamp, product, bid_price_1..3, bid_volume_1..3, ask_price_1..3, ask_volume_1..3, mid_price, profit_and_loss
tradestimestamp, buyer, seller, symbol, currency, price, quantity
observations, P3timestamp, bidPrice, askPrice, transportFees, exportTariff, importTariff, sugarPrice, sunlightIndex
observations, P2 round 2timestamp, ORCHIDS, TRANSPORT_FEES, EXPORT_TARIFF, IMPORT_TARIFF, SUNLIGHT, HUMIDITY, DAY

Timestamps step by 100 within a day, so one day is 10,000 iterations. Ask volumes in the order book are negative in the trader's OrderDepth, positive in these files.

Where the files come from

Prosperity 1 to 3 and Prosperity 4 round 1 are the Library's own copies, pinned to commit 79108e33 of MarkBrezina/Ctrl-Alt-DefeatTheMarket. The Prosperity 2 and 3 round 1 files match, byte for byte, the copies bundled with Jasper van Merle's backtesters. Prosperity 4's other rounds link to a community backtester at commit 40671cab.

Neither source records reuse terms for the competition data itself, so check before you redistribute it. The profile behind this page is profile.json, produced by ctrl_research.py.
