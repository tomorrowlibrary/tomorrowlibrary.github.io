Chapter 03 / Inventory, portfolio & risk

Manage what you hold.

Connect individual trades to the position they create. Plan how inventory, portfolio exposure and risk limits shape every decision.

The core question

What happens to your risk if every outstanding order fills?

In this chapter.

01Track inventory & capacity

A quote changes the position you may end up holding. Record current inventory and consider outstanding orders before deciding what to submit next.

  • Track inventory separately for each product.
  • Evaluate possible fills on both sides of the book.
  • Use the published edition rules for position limits and order handling.
02Think at portfolio level

Separate strategies can create the same underlying exposure. Look beyond the profit of one trade to the way products and strategies behave together.

  • Monitor concentration and related positions.
  • Give strategies explicit capacity or risk budgets.
  • Treat a hedge as an imperfect relationship that can change.
03Make the controls explicit

Risk rules need to become observable checks. Decide in advance how the system should respond to missing data, invalid orders, extreme inventory and an unexpected loss.

  • Check limits before orders are submitted.
  • Track PnL, drawdowns and the time spent near position limits.
  • Define what should reduce exposure or pause a strategy.
