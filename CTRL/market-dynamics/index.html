Chapter 02 / Market dynamics & alpha

Understand the market.

Explore how prices, liquidity and trading activity behave. Turn observations into testable ideas, with the failure conditions written down.

The core question

Why might this pattern persist, and when might it fail?

In this chapter.

01Read the market mechanics

Start with bid and ask prices, available depth, the flow of trades and the information supplied by the round. These observations give a hypothesis its context.

  • Distinguish the quoted spread from the spread you can capture.
  • Study liquidity and price changes across different market conditions.
  • Check which mechanics are specific to the historical edition.
02Explore simple alpha hypotheses

Mean reversion, momentum, relative value and order-flow signals are useful starting hypotheses. Each needs evidence on data that was not used to choose the idea.

  • Define the signal, expected direction and prediction horizon.
  • Compare with an unconditional or simple rule-based baseline.
  • Separate a prediction about price from a strategy that can trade it.
03Describe the risks & failure modes

An attractive historical pattern can depend on a narrow market condition or a convenient fill assumption. Investigate the periods in which the hypothesis stops working.

  • Check sensitivity to parameters and the chosen sample.
  • Inspect adverse selection: what happens after your quote is filled?
  • Test whether an apparent relationship survives a change in regime.

A hypothesis card
