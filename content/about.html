The purpose

Tomorrow Library brings together the material needed to learn and practise quantitative finance: mathematical foundations, research procedures, reusable code, datasets, papers and competition work. It is organised by the reader's task so the material can be found and used without knowing which repository originally held it.

What is included

The repository map connects Ctrl-Alt-DefeatTheMarket, Knowledgebase, Foundational Quantitative Research, TMRW, WTech and KB. The source collections have different purposes and maturity levels; the Library identifies those differences and provides edited entry points.

How to read the status labels

Runnable workbench identifies the included reference package and its tests. Historical source points to an earlier implementation or notebook. Research draft means an open formulation or provisional argument, not a demonstrated result. External solutions remain attributed to their authors.

Continuity with Tomorrow Quant Archive

The original Tomorrow Quant Archive supplied the starting orientation, learning paths, community directory and competition collection. Its source has been recovered. The original personal foreword is preserved as a historical essay, while the new homepage provides a practical entry point into the material.
