Prerequisites

Probability distributions, estimation, discounting and basic insurance contracts.

What to study

Study frequency/severity models, survival and hazard functions, claim development and cash-flow timing. Keep process uncertainty separate from uncertainty about fitted parameters.

A project to complete

Build a small frequency/severity loss simulation. Document the assumptions, compare simulated and analytical moments where possible, then examine sensitivity to tail and dependency assumptions.

A useful deliverable

Publish the problem definition, documented data, assumptions, reproducible code, a simple comparison and a short discussion of failure cases. Label demonstrations and provisional findings clearly.
