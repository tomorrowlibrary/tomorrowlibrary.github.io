Prerequisites

Python, time series, market orders, limit orders and basic account arithmetic.

What to study

Study data quality, information timing, signal evaluation, transaction costs, inventory and risk limits. Begin with an interpretable baseline and keep the forecasting and execution questions distinct.

A project to complete

Run the workbench on a documented price series, record a chronological comparison and inspect the trade ledger. Then compare a market and limit order in Tomorrow Markets.

A useful deliverable

Publish the problem definition, documented data, assumptions, reproducible code, a simple comparison and a short discussion of failure cases. Label demonstrations and provisional findings clearly.
