Prerequisites

Probability, regression, accounting basics and a careful understanding of the outcome definition.

What to study

Study probability of default, loss given default and exposure at default. Distinguish a predictive score from a calibrated probability and a realised loss from an expected loss.

A project to complete

Create a time-split default model on a dataset with documented definitions. Compare calibration and discrimination, check missingness and explain which observations are available at the decision time.

A useful deliverable

Publish the problem definition, documented data, assumptions, reproducible code, a simple comparison and a short discussion of failure cases. Label demonstrations and provisional findings clearly.
