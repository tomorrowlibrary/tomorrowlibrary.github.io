Prerequisites

Financial statements, valuation, statistics and data provenance.

What to study

Translate qualitative reasoning into explicit variables and testable predictions. Align a financial period with its publication date, and track revisions rather than giving a historical decision the final corrected value.

A project to complete

Form one economic hypothesis using public releases. Record availability times, create a simple ranking or valuation rule and test incremental evidence against a baseline.

A useful deliverable

Publish the problem definition, documented data, assumptions, reproducible code, a simple comparison and a short discussion of failure cases. Label demonstrations and provisional findings clearly.
