Prerequisites

Probability, position accounting, portfolio weights and scenario analysis.

What to study

Study volatility, correlation, drawdowns, tail losses, sensitivities and stress tests. Link each metric to a concrete control: position limits, loss budgets, liquidity constraints or an escalation rule.

A project to complete

Reconstruct an account's drawdown and historical tail losses. Add a concentration cap, compare before/after exposure and stress a joint move that is worse than the calibration sample.

A useful deliverable

Publish the problem definition, documented data, assumptions, reproducible code, a simple comparison and a short discussion of failure cases. Label demonstrations and provisional findings clearly.
