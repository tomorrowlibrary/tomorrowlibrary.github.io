Prerequisites

Returns, compounding, covariance and point-in-time asset universes.

What to study

Study weighting, rebalancing, factor exposures, turnover, drawdowns and benchmark selection. Include survivorship and corporate-action treatment in the data design.

A project to complete

Compare equal weights with an inverse-volatility rule using training-only volatility estimates. Preserve cash, apply costs, and evaluate both on the same subsequent dates.

A useful deliverable

Publish the problem definition, documented data, assumptions, reproducible code, a simple comparison and a short discussion of failure cases. Label demonstrations and provisional findings clearly.
